About

I live in Little Rock and spend most of my brain cycles on networks, old carrier gear, and the software that talks to it. Formal training is an Associate of Applied Science in Computer Networking. Day-to-day that shows up as wiring, troubleshooting, and building small tools that actually get used — not slideware.

On the field side I did home and light-commercial consumer-electronics and appliance installs in the Best Buy orbit through ING/Manada (working with people like Scott Hall locally): the jobs Geek Squad doesn’t take — custom surfaces, hardwired dishwashers, coax that has to survive a real bend radius. That work stuck. You learn fast what “don’t kink the coax” means when you’re the one pulling it.

Outside paid work I keep a lab of Windows and Linux boxes, dig through U-verse / DirecTV / Echostar dump formats, watch local fire-EMS radio, and run Discord/LAN tooling for friends and community servers. If it has a serial header, a proprietary filesystem, or a dusty PDF from Bell Labs, I’m interested.
